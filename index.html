📢 Alliance Rules & Guidelines

Welcome to VEN! 💙
Our goal is to build a strong, friendly, and active alliance where everyone enjoys the game and grows together.

🤝 Respect & Teamwork

  • Treat all alliance members with respect.
  • No drama, harassment, or toxic behavior.
  • Follow leadership instructions during alliance events and wars.
  • Help fellow members whenever possible.
  • Most importantly... Have Fun! 🎮

📅 Activity

  • Stay active and participate in alliance events.
  • If you'll be inactive for an extended period, please inform the Leadership Team.
  • Contribute to the alliance whenever possible.

⚔️ Combat Rules

Alliance Members

  • Do NOT attack alliance members in the Arena.

NAP Rules

  • Do NOT scout or attack members of NAP alliances or their academy alliances.

Non-NAP Rules

  • Maximum 3 city hits per player.
  • No rallies on cities.

Forbidden

  • Do NOT attack alliance banners or buildings.
  • Unsportsmanlike behavior will not be tolerated.

👑 Alliance Rank Structure

Our ranks are based on activity, responsibility, and contribution. Every role is important in helping our alliance grow stronger together.

👑 R5 — Alliance Leader

  • Leads the alliance and sets its direction.
  • Makes major alliance decisions.
  • Oversees strategy and diplomacy.

🛡️ R4 — Leadership Team

  • Supports the Alliance Leader.
  • Assists with recruitment, member management, event coordination, and alliance development.
  • Helps maintain a positive and active community.

⚔️ R3 — Veterans

  • Experienced and trusted members.
  • Lead rallies during Bear Trap and other alliance events.
  • Guide newer members and provide gameplay support.

🤝 R2 — Members

  • Active members who participate in alliance events.
  • Continue growing, learning, and contributing to the alliance.
  • Join rallies and support alliance activities.

🌱 R1 — Recruits

  • New members getting familiar with the alliance.
  • Members inactive for more than 2 days may be moved to R1.
  • Continued inactivity without notice may result in removal to make room for active players.
Every rank plays an important role in our success. Stay active, support one another, and let's continue building a stronger alliance together! 💪

🐻 Bear Trap Guide

Bear Trap is an alliance event where members work together to defeat the Bear by hosting and joining rallies. The more damage your alliance deals, the better the rewards for everyone.

🎁 Rewards

  • Personal rewards based on the damage you deal.
  • Alliance milestone rewards as the alliance reaches higher Bear levels.
  • Better performance means better chests and more rewards for everyone.

⚔️ Rally Roles

👑 R5 / 🛡️ R4 / ⚔️ R3

  • Start (host) rallies.
  • Hosting rallies gives more personal points because your strongest heroes lead the attack.
  • More rallies = More alliance damage = Better rewards for everyone.

🤝 R2 / 🌱 R1

  • Join rallies started by R3, R4 and R5.
  • Help fill rallies quickly to maximize alliance damage.

🐻 Bear Trap Rules

🔵 Rally Host

  • Use your best 3 heroes.
  • No blue heroes.

🔵 Rally Joiners

Use ONE of these heroes as your first hero:

  • Amadeus + Hero + Hero
  • Chenko + Hero + Hero
  • Yeonwoo + Hero + Hero
  • Amane + Hero + Hero

If you don't have any of these heroes, join without heroes.

🔴 Important

  • Rally hosts should remove members using the wrong heroes.
  • Joiners can simply rejoin with the correct setup.
💙 Let's work together, fill rallies quickly, and maximize everyone's rewards!

🛡️ Viking Attack Guide

Viking Attack is a 20-wave alliance PvE event where Vikings attack alliance members and the Alliance Headquarters (HQ). Success depends on teamwork, proper reinforcements, and following the battle strategy.

Unlike many events, you earn most of your points by reinforcing other members, not by defending your own city.

🎁 Rewards

  • 🏆 Personal Ranking Rewards
  • 🎁 Alliance Milestone Rewards
  • 💎 Resources and valuable items
  • 📈 Event Points based on your contribution

🤝 Reinforcement Strategy

  • Send Infantry only when reinforcing members.
  • Do NOT send Archers when reinforcing players. Archers do not earn reinforcement points.

🏰 HQ Defense (Rounds 10 & 20)

  • Reinforce the Alliance HQ immediately.
  • Use your best heroes.
  • Send 100% Infantry & Cavalry (or follow leadership instructions).
  • Save a troop formation of approximately 43K highest-tier troops before the event.

⚔️ Swordland Showdown

Fight. Capture. Defend. Dominate.

Swordland Showdown is a large-scale PvP battlefield where alliances compete to capture objectives, defeat enemies, and earn valuable rewards.

📋 Pre-Event Checklist

  • Select ONE Legion (Legion 1 or Legion 2) and tap Vote under Deployment.
  • For the other Legion, choose Abstention.
  • Only register if you can participate for the full event.

⭐ Quick Tips & Tactics

  • Reach 180,000 Personal Points to unlock top personal rewards.
  • Defend assigned objectives (Sanctums, Sword Shrine, Royal Stables, Bell Tower).
  • Join rallies using Chenko.

⚡ F2P Survival Tricks

❤️ Free Hospital Trick

  • No permanent troop deaths occur during Swordland.
  • If your hospital fills up, leave the battlefield and your troops heal instantly.
  • Re-enter after the 12-minute cooldown.

🏕️ Encampment Trick

  • Send all troops to an Encampment and leave your city empty. Your troops remain safe!
💙 Strong players win battles. Strong alliances win wars.

Fight Smart ⚔️ • Defend Together 🛡️ • Capture Objectives 🏰 • Reach 180K 🏆
